Settld

Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Settld splits shared expenses for trips, flats and small groups. It is made by Gaurav Kumar, The Algothrim, in New Delhi, India. This page says what Settld keeps, where it lives, who can see it and how to delete it. Questions go to gaurav@thealgothrim.com.

On your device first

Everything you add lives on your phone or computer: your groups, the people in them, expenses, payments you record, receipts and payment proof you attach, the history of edits, and your profile (your name, and your UPI ID and phone number if you add them). If you never sign in, none of it leaves your device.

If you sign in with Google

  • Your account: your email address, name and profile picture from Google.
  • A private backup of the ledger above, receipts included, so it survives a lost phone and follows you to another device. Only you can read it.

If you share a group

Turning on sharing moves that one group to a space its members can reach: its name, the people in it (names and UPI IDs), expenses, payments, receipts and history. Every member can see and add to it. Anyone signed in who holds the invite link can see the group's name and its people (names and UPI IDs) before joining, so they can pick which one is them; only members see the expenses, payments and receipts. Your other groups stay private.

What Settld never does

No ads, no analytics and no tracking. Settld never asks for bank logins, card numbers or UPI PINs, and never moves money: "Pay via UPI" opens your own UPI app with the amount filled in, and the payment happens there.

Where it lives

  • Google Firebase holds accounts, backups and shared groups, in Google's Mumbai region (asia-south1).
  • Vercel serves the app. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

These providers process data only to run Settld. Everything travels over encrypted connections, and the database's security rules keep each backup to its owner and each shared group to its members.

Keeping and deleting it

  • You > Export ledger downloads everything as a file at any time.
  • You > Erase all data wipes this device, and your backup too if you are signed in.
  • You > Delete account leaves your shared groups, deletes groups nobody else joined, erases your backup, then deletes your login. In a group other people still use, their ledger stays; your name stays on the entries it's part of, no longer linked to any account.

If you can't open the app, see how to ask for deletion by email. Settld does not sell, rent or share your data, and would disclose it only if Indian law required it.

Children

Settld is meant for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. Changes that affect what is kept will also be announced in the app.
