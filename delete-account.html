Delete your Settld account

Settld is made by The Algothrim. You can delete your account and your data whenever you like.

In the app

  1. Open Settld and go to You.
  2. Tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm.

If you last signed in a while ago, Google asks you to confirm it's you first. Want a copy before you go? You > Export ledger downloads everything as a file.

By email, if you can't open the app

Write to gaurav@thealgothrim.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Settld account". Your account and data are deleted within 7 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your backup: groups, expenses, payments, receipts, history and profile
  • Shared groups that nobody else joined, with everything in them
  • Your login

What stays with other people

In a shared group other people still use, you leave it and their ledger stays whole, because it's theirs too. Your name remains on the expenses and payments you were part of, no longer linked to any account. Nothing else about you is kept.
